How to install

Requirements: GTA SA 1.0 US, MoonLoader 0.26+, SAMPFUNCS, ImGui, and samp.events.

  1. Click the download button above to download the file.
  2. Extract the 7z archive and place DiamondMenu.lua into your GTA San Andreas\moonloader\ folder.
  3. Connect to HZG (samp.hzgaming.net:7777) and type /diamondmenu.
